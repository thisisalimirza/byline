Help

Short answers for credits, articles, and refunds. Your drafts stay in this browser until accounts are available.

Credits

One credit writes one article. Packs are 10 for $5, 25 for $10, or 75 for $25. Open Buy from the credits control in the app. Credits do not expire.

Bought on another device? Restore my credits with the checkout email. We email a one-time code, then the credits show up in this browser.

Failed generations

If an article fails on our side, the credit is returned automatically. You do not need to ask. Start the article again when you are ready.

Downloads and exports

After an article finishes, use Copy, Preview, or the PDF control in the app to take it with you. Drafts on this browser can be saved as a file here.

Receipts and invoices

Stripe emails a receipt when you pay. After you verify your purchase email on the refund page, each pack includes a receipt link.

Refunds

A full refund is available within 14 days if you have not used the pack. After that, unused credits are refunded pro-rata for 30 days from the purchase. Each pack can be refunded once, back to the original payment. Do it yourself on the refund page.

Deleting data

Delete drafts on this browser with the button above. To delete the customer profile tied to a purchase email, verify that email on the refund page and choose Delete my data. Payment records stay with Stripe for accounting.

You can also download purchase data after verifying your email.

Contact

Use this if the pages above do not solve it. Messages go to ali@braskgroup.com.
